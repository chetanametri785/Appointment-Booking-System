Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking and visiting ApexCare.

How do I check the status of my appointment?

Click on "Track Status" in the top navigation or enter your unique Booking Reference (e.g., APT-2026-101) or phone number in our tracking tool to view status, reschedule, or print your pass.

Can I reschedule or cancel my appointment?

Yes! You can reschedule or cancel at any time directly through the "Track Status" portal without any cancellation penalty.

What do I need to bring for my appointment?

Please bring a valid photo ID, your insurance card (if applicable), and your digital appointment pass or reference code. Please arrive 10 minutes prior to your scheduled time.
